About Maestro

A little story with love for good coffee & food

Who We Are

Just Cafe started as a small dream to bring people together over a perfect cup of filter kaapi and hot snacks. We believe that good food and great conversations can make any day better.

What We Love

  • Authentic South Indian filter coffee
  • Traditional idli, dosa, vada & pongal
  • Spiced chai varieties - adrak, masala, honey
  • Freshly made snacks every morning
  • A warm, friendly place to relax

Our Promise

We use fresh ingredients, make everything with care, and always keep our space clean and welcoming. Whether you're here for a quick coffee or a long chat with friends — you're always welcome at Maestro.
